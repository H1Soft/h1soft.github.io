What is Mongle's MBTI-style test?

Mongle is an independently created 16-type personality test that explores your preferences through everyday choices. Meet the characters from the mobile app in your browser. It's free, with no download or account needed.

Choose 12 or 40 questions

Try the 12-question speed test for a quick introduction, or choose the 40-question full test to explore more situations. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.

Four dimensions, 16 personality types

Your answers explore four preference pairs: E–I, S–N, T–F, and J–P. Your result shows each dimension alongside the Mongle character for your type.

Meet your character and share your result

Read your character's personality description, share a result link, or save an image. Mongle is not the official MBTI assessment and does not provide a psychological diagnosis. Enjoy it as a playful way to reflect on yourself.
