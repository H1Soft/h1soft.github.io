Ongle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

1. General

This policy applies to the Ongle hwp and hwpx viewer app ("the app") from H1Soft ("the team"). The team complies with applicable law, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

2. On-device by design

Viewing, full-document search, table export and PDF or image conversion all run on your device. Document files, their contents, file names and metadata are never sent to a server, and the team cannot access them.

Core features work with the network fully blocked.

3. What we collect

The app collects no information that identifies you — no name, contact details or account. There is no sign-up and no login.

4. Stored on your device

The following is stored only in app-private storage.

  • Recent documents — the path of files you opened and your last reading position
  • Conversion output — PDFs, images and table files you chose to save
  • App settings — display mode, reading mode, language

5. Permissions

Permissions are used only for the purposes below.

  • Files and storage — only to open a document you pick and save conversion output where you choose. Files you have not selected are never accessed

6. Optional diagnostics (opt-in)

To improve stability, the following is collected only if you explicitly opt in. Consent is requested on a dedicated screen at first launch and can be withdrawn instantly in settings.

  • Crash stack traces
  • On a render failure, structural statistics about the document — record tag distribution, document version, page count
  • Document text and images are never collected.

7. File reports (opt-in)

If you use the feature that reports an unopenable document to the team, the file itself is sent to the team. This is stated plainly on the report screen and requires your explicit checkbox consent.

Reported files are used only to diagnose the cause, are deleted as soon as analysis is done, and are never shared with third parties. Reporting is entirely optional.

8. Cloud AI features

Where cloud-based AI features exist, they are off by default and require per-feature consent. You see a preview of what would be transmitted, and transmitted content is not retained after the response. On-device AI features transmit nothing.

9. Sharing, processors, overseas transfer

Because the app collects no personal information, there is no sharing and no processor. No advertising SDK is included. If you opt into diagnostics, that data may be transferred to a crash-reporting provider's overseas servers; the recipient and the items are named on the consent screen.

10. Deletion

On-device data is removed when you clear the recent documents list or uninstall the app. The team holds no copy.

11. Your rights

The team stores no personal information, so there is no team-held record subject to access, correction or deletion. Diagnostics consent can be withdrawn in settings at any time.

12. Children

The app collects no personal information from any user, including children under 14.

14. Changes

When this privacy policy changes, we post the change and its effective date on this page and in the app. Changes unfavourable to users are announced at least 30 days before they take effect.
