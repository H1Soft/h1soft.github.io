FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are my hwp files uploaded?

No. Viewing, search, table export and PDF conversion all run on your device — neither the document nor its file name leaves it.

Which formats are supported?

hwp 5.0 and hwpx. HML is supported for verified structures only, and password- or DRM-protected distribution documents cannot be opened.

Is this related to Hancom?

Not at all. Ongle is independent software with no affiliation with, sponsorship from or approval by Hancom Inc., and it uses none of their logos or branding.
