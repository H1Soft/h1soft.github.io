HWP · HWPX · HML

Open HWP & HWPX. Save as PDF.

Read Korean documents on your iPhone, extract tables to Excel and save pages as PDF. Ongle is an on-device viewer for HWP 5.0, HWPX and HML files.

Coming to iPhone

Tools for your documents

  • Opens hwp, hwpx and hml documents
  • Saves as PDF
  • Extracts tables to Excel (XLSX) and CSV, with a choice of how merged cells are written
  • Searches the whole document, including text inside tables and footnotes
  • Builds an outline automatically and shows page thumbnails
  • Dark mode with paper inversion for reading at night
  • Cuts pages, exports images, extracts plain text
  • Copies equations as LaTeX
  • Opens password-protected documents
  • Fills in form fields

Fonts and complex document elements may display differently from the original. PDF conversion varies by document; some output is image-based. HWP 3.0 and DRM-protected documents are not supported.
