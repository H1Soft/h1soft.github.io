FAQ

What is H1Soft?

H1Soft is a mobile app team building iOS and Android apps from a single codebase with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform. Our goal is to make everyday tools as fast and simple as possible.

Is QR Scanner free?

Yes, QR Scanner is free. Scanning, creating, and history are all available at no cost.

Is my scan history stored on a server?

No. All scan and creation history is stored locally on your device only, and is never sent to or collected by any server.

What kinds of QR codes can I create?

16 types in total: link, text, phone, location, email, SMS, Wi-Fi and calendar QR codes, plus social QR codes for Facebook, X, YouTube, Instagram, KakaoTalk, Line and more.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, creating, and history all work perfectly without a network connection.
