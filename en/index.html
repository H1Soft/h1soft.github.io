FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of apps does H1Soft make?

Two lines: everyday utilities (QR Scanner, Seukscan, Ongle) built fast and simple, and lifestyle apps (Skinping, LoLdating) built for the long run with their own brands. Everything runs on Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform.

Where can I see Skinping and LoLdating?

Each has its own landing page — the lifestyle cards above link straight to them (currently Korean-only).

Is utility app data stored on a server?

No. The utility line processes everything locally — scan and creation history stays on your device.

Is QR Scanner free?

Yes. QR Scanner is free, with every feature included.

What does the team build with?

Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform — one codebase for iOS and Android. Ongle also uses a Rust engine.
