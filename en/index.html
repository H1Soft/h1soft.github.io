FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of team is H1Soft?

A mobile development team building cross-platform apps for iOS and Android with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform — one codebase, native performance on both.

Is QR Scanner free?

Yes. QR Scanner is free, with every feature included.

Is my scan history stored on a server?

No. Everything is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server.

What kinds of QR codes can I create?

General codes — link, text, phone, location, email, SMS, Wi-Fi, calendar — plus social codes for Facebook, X, YouTube, Instagram, KakaoTalk, Line and more.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, creating and history all work without a network connection.
