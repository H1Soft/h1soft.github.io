FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is H1Soft?

H1Soft is an IT technology startup that builds mobile apps. We ship iOS and Android from a single codebase with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform, across three product lines: utilities, lifestyle and games.

What kind of apps does H1Soft make?

Three lines: everyday utilities (QR Scanner, Seukscan, Ongle) built fast and simple, lifestyle apps (Mongle, Skinping, LoLdating) built for the long run with their own brands, and a games line that starts with Sudoku Voyage. Everything runs on Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform.

Where can I see Mongle, Skinping and LoLdating?

Each has its own landing page — the lifestyle cards above link straight to them.

Is utility app data stored on a server?

No. The utility line processes everything locally — scan and creation history stays on your device.

What is Sudoku Voyage?

A sudoku game where every puzzle you solve moves you along a route around the world — 12 regions with 20 stages each, six technique-based difficulties, and a three-step Smart Hint that shows the way before the answer. No account, fully offline.

What does the team build with?

Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform — one codebase for iOS and Android. Ongle also uses a Rust engine.
