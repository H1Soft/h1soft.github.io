SCREENS

This is what the app looks like.

A travel journal, carried into the app — resume from home in three seconds, open the next region on the map, and browse the postcards you have collected.

01 · Home

Back in the puzzle in three seconds.

Open the app and the resume card sits at the top — with the daily puzzle and your progress in the current region on the same screen.

02 · Game

Pick a cell and the path shows itself.

Its row, column and box light up along with every matching number. Undo, erase, notes and hints sit right under the board.

03 · Dark mode

A genuinely dark board, for solving at night.

In mistake-limited mode a cell that breaks the rules is flagged where you put it. The count left for each number rides under the pad as a ring.

04 · Album

Every postcard you have earned.

They arrive as line art and fill with color as you collect stars. Filter by region, and see what it takes to open the ones still empty.

05 · Daily

One puzzle a day, one stamp.

The monthly calendar shows each day's difficulty. Missed days can be played retroactively, and shields keep a streak alive.

06 · Academy

Where the techniques are taught.

The first time a new technique appears on your voyage, the app offers a three-minute lesson — declining costs you nothing. Fourteen in order.

07 · Postcard earned

The moment the color fills in.

Clear a landmark stage and a postcard is yours. Keep it in the album, or share it on the spot.

08 · Passport

A record of where you have been.

Stamps for clearing a region, for streaks, for finishing without a mistake. Some stay hidden until you meet the condition.

Design mockups of the app. Details may change in the released build.

FAQ

Before you ask.

More answers — including backup and device transfer — on the support page.

What is locked?

Every difficulty, theme, hint and collectible opens by playing. Nothing gates your progress.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Puzzle generation and your records are handled on device, so airplane mode changes nothing.

Which devices are supported?

Android 8.0 and above, iOS 16 and above.

Does progress sync between iOS and Android?

No. There is no account, so nothing syncs automatically. Move a backup file across instead.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Export a backup in Settings and import it on the new device. There is no automatic backup, so export before you switch.

Do puzzles always have one solution?

Yes. Every puzzle is verified for a unique solution when it is generated.

Where do I report a problem?

Email us at h1.soft.x001@gmail.com.
