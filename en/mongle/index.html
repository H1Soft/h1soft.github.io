Frequently asked questions

Can I take the test on the web?

No — the test lives in the app. The character reveal and the result card are made for a phone screen. Install it and you are done in a minute.

Do I need an account?

No. Install and start. We never ask for an email or a phone number.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Mongle has no server, so even we have no way to see your results.

Do I lose my results if I change phones?

Export your data to a file in Settings, then import it on the new one. There is no server, so there is no automatic backup — export before you wipe the old phone.

Is this test scientifically accurate?

No. Mongle is entertainment, not a psychological assessment. Take it lightly.

Is the app available in English?

The app UI is Korean for now. English is on the roadmap, without a fixed date — we would rather say so than promise a month we cannot keep.
