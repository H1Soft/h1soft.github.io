Features

1-minute quick test, 5-minute precise test

Twelve questions give you one of 16 personality types. Want more questions? The 40-question precise test scores every axis — E/I, S/N, T/F, J/P — as a percentage gauge. Every result is saved, so you get a timeline of how your type shifts over time.

16 characters, one for each type

Every type has its own pastel character with its own face and mood. Your result page covers your style in love, in friendship, at school and at work, the lines you say too often, plus your best match and your chaos match.

Personality compatibility

Score any two types and see the breakdown: conversation flow, planning style, energy sync, and what actually happens if the two of you travel together. Add two friends and the group compatibility matrix and team chemistry read unlock.

Friend codes

Add friends with a six-character code (XXX-XXX) or a QR scan. The code carries only a type and four percentages. It never leaves your device unless you send it yourself.

Something new every day

A daily fortune with a lucky item and lucky color, a balance game shared by every type, and a 30-second mini quiz. Keep a check-in streak, with freezes so a missed day doesn't hurt.

A collection of 32 characters

Unlock characters by testing, adding friends, checking compatibility and showing up daily. Some are hidden.

Shareable result cards

Dress your result up with pastel gradient, checkered, sticker-book or film-frame themes, then post it to a story or send it to a chat.

No server, ever

No account, no sign-in, no tracking. Every record lives on your phone and everything works in airplane mode. Changing phones? Export and import your data as JSON.

Privacy policy

Mongle works without an account, advertising or analytics servers. The developer does not collect or receive personal data from the app. Results, nicknames, friends, settings and collection progress are stored on your device.

Support

How long does the test take?

The quick test is 12 questions, about a minute. The precise test is 40 questions, about five.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every feature works in airplane mode.

Is this the official MBTI assessment?

No. Mongle is an original 16-type personality quiz made for fun.
