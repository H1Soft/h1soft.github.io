Mongle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

1. General

This policy applies to the Mongle app ("the app") and the Mongle landing page, provided by H1Soft ("the team"). The team complies with applicable law, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

2. In three lines

  • Mongle has no server. No sign-up, no login.
  • Test results and your friend list live only on your device.
  • So the team cannot see that data — there is no path by which we could.

3. What we never collect

The app collects no personal information at all: no name, email, phone number, location or advertising identifier. There is no server to receive it.

4. Stored on your device

The following is written only to device storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • Test results and type history
  • Friend list and compatibility lookups
  • Collection progress and check-in records
  • App settings — notifications, display mode

5. Sharing

Since nothing is collected, nothing is shared. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

6. Children's data

Mongle collects no personal information from users of any age, so it collects none from children under 14 either.

7. How to delete your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything. You can also erase it immediately from Settings > Reset all data in the app.

There is no server, so there is no automatic backup. Export your data to a file in Settings before switching devices.

8. About this website

The Mongle landing page sets no cookies and has no form that takes a name or email. Any visit statistics are processed only in aggregate, without identifying individuals; the H1Soft website privacy policy governs the details.

9. Your rights

The team stores no personal information, so there is no team-held record subject to access, correction or deletion. You control all on-device data directly.

10. Changes

When this privacy policy changes, we post the change and its effective date on this page and in the app. Changes unfavourable to users are announced at least 30 days before they take effect.

11. Contact

For questions about privacy, email h1.soft.x001@gmail.com. We reply within 7 business days.
