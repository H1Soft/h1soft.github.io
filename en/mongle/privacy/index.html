Mongle · Privacy policy

Mongle works without an account, advertising or analytics servers. The developer does not collect or receive personal data from the app. Results, nicknames, friends, settings and collection progress are stored on your device.

The camera is used only when you scan a friend’s QR code. Video is not saved or sent. Permission to add photos is used only to save a result card to Photos.

Friend codes, card images and JSON backups are sent to the app you choose only when you share them. If you email support, your address and message are used to answer your request. Please omit sensitive information.

Reset your data in Settings or delete the app. Delete exported files and saved photos separately. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your backup settings.

This website has no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and connection information to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

The app is entertainment, not a professional psychological assessment. Privacy contact: h1.soft.x001@gmail.com. Updated: 2026-09-07.
