Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

H1Soft ("the team") values the privacy of users of the QR Scanner app ("the app") and complies with applicable laws. This policy applies to the app and the website (h1soft.github.io).

1. Personal data we collect

The team does not collect any personal data.

  • The app has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • QR scan and creation history is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to or collected by any server.

2. App permissions

  • Camera: used only for scanning QR codes.
  • Photos (gallery): used only for recognizing QR codes from images and saving QR codes you create.

Data accessed through these permissions never leaves your device.

3. Third-party sharing and processing

Since no personal data is collected, no third-party sharing or outsourced processing takes place.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can delete scan and creation records directly from the app's history. Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device.

5. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

6. Contact

Privacy inquiries: contact@h1soft.app
