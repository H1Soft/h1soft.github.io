Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

1. General

H1Soft ("the team") respects the privacy of visitors to h1soft.github.io ("this site") and complies with applicable law, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

This policy covers this website only. Each app has its own privacy policy.

2. What we collect

This site has no sign-up and does not directly collect information that identifies you. The following may be processed automatically.

  • Visit statistics — timestamp, page path, browser and device type, referrer and cookie identifiers, collected by Google Analytics 4 after consent
  • Contact email — the sender address and body of any mail you choose to send us

3. Purpose and retention

Collected information is used only for the purposes below.

  • Visit statistics: improving the site — processed according to the Analytics property’s data-retention settings
  • Contact email: answering your enquiry — kept for one year after the enquiry is closed, then deleted

4. Sharing and overseas transfer

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. This site does rely on the overseas providers below, and access records may be transferred outside Korea while the site operates.

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — static hosting (GitHub Pages), access logs, retained for the duration of the service
  • Google LLC (USA) — consent-based Google Analytics visit statistics, cookies and device identifiers
  • jsDelivr / Cloudflare (overseas) — web font delivery, access IP, processed only at request time

5. Cookies

Analytics cookies are used only after you allow them, with a configured lifetime of 180 days. You may decline through Analytics settings. Language and consent choices are stored in browser localStorage. See our analytics collection and withdrawal details.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time using the contact below.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and the team keeps two-factor authentication and access control on the contact mailbox.

8. Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

9. Changes

When this privacy policy changes, we post the change and its effective date on this page. Changes unfavourable to users are announced at least 30 days before they take effect.

10. Contact

For questions about privacy, email h1.soft.x001@gmail.com. We reply within 7 business days.
