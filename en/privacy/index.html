Effective date: August 11, 2026

1. General

H1Soft ("the team") values the privacy of QR Scanner and website users and complies with applicable laws. This policy applies to the QR Scanner app and the h1soft website.

The camera is used only to recognize QR codes. Camera footage is never stored or sent anywhere.
