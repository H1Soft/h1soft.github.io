What is different

The draw, the paper, the album

The fingertip comes first

The rectangle you drag follows your finger exactly. Ink deepens as the count closes in, and a short tap answers the moment it matches.

One material: paper

Everything on screen could be made from paper, pencil, ink and a stamp. No glow, no confetti.

What you solve stays with you

Your finished division is printed as a stamp. Colour starts from the rectangle you solved last, so the same puzzle looks different in every album.

FAQ

What people ask first

What is shikaku?

A logic puzzle: divide a grid of numbers into rectangles. Each rectangle has as many cells as its number and holds exactly one number. It was devised in Japan in 1989.

Does it work offline?

Solving, the album and your records all run on the device.

Does using a hint show up?

That sheet is recorded as assisted and the postmark grade changes. Nothing blocks your progress.

Where is my progress stored?

On the device, and optionally synced to your other devices through iCloud or Google Play.

Which languages?

한국어 · English · 日本語.

Which devices?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets.
