FAQ

Do you have any questions?

Is it free?

Yes. The web test, compatibility results and collection are free. The app is still in development.

What is the difference between an app and the web?

You can take the test and meet your plush on the web now. A plush room, friends map, lock screen wallpapers and home screen widgets are planned for the app. App screens shown here are design previews.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. This is a playful self-reflection test, not a substitute for a medical or psychological diagnosis.

Can my result change?

Attachment styles can change with your relationship experiences. Different results in romance and friendship modes are natural.

What about my privacy?

No sign-in is required, and we don't ask for your name or contact details. Test results and collections are stored on your device. Shared links may contain a nickname and result information, so check before posting them publicly.

How do I meet the secret plush?

It appears when both your raw anxiety and avoidance scores are either 10 or 11. Your answers determine the result; it isn't random. The roughly 1.1% figure is a simulation estimate, not a measured rate among real users.
