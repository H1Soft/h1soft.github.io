# Nonogram Trip official website

Platform: responsive static website. Stack: Astro, TypeScript modules and CSS, hosted at https://h1soft.github.io/nonogram/.
Audience: English and Korean puzzle players exploring a calm, offline travel nonogram app. The website introduces the app through city artwork, an album preview and a real gameplay video.
Product truth: 12 cities, 192 campaign puzzles, free features, Android 8+ and iOS 15+, local progress and manual backups. Store listings are not yet supplied, so availability is clearly marked coming soon. No visitor analytics is enabled without a configured provider.
Scope: bilingual landing, privacy, terms, support, city themes, a photographic album gallery and development interaction; integration with the existing H1Soft website.
