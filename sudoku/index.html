Sudoku Voyage is classic sudoku — the 9×9 number logic puzzle — turned into a journey around the world.

Solve a puzzle, clear a stage, and move to the next city on your route. Lisbon, Marrakesh, Istanbul, Kyoto, Busan and Sydney wait along the way: 120 stages in all, with landmark stages that leave a postcard in your album.

A VOYAGE, NOT A LIST OF GRIDS
• A world map instead of an endless puzzle list
• 6 cities, 20 stages each, 24 postcards to collect
• Postcards gain color as you earn ★3 — full color and gold foil at the end
• A traveler's passport that stamps your milestones
• Board themes you unlock by finishing a region

CLASSIC SUDOKU, DONE PROPERLY
• Six difficulties: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master
• Every puzzle is generated with exactly one solution
• Difficulty is graded by the hardest technique a puzzle actually needs
• Pencil notes, smart note cleanup, auto-fill notes
• Cell-first or digit-first input — whichever suits you
• Undo, erase, mistake highlighting, and a timer you can hide

LEARN THE TECHNIQUES
• An Academy covering Full House, Naked Single, Hidden Single, Pointing Pair, Claiming, Naked Pair, Hidden Pair, Naked Triple, X-Wing, Swordfish, XY-Wing and Simple Chains
• Short guided lessons with practice puzzles
• Three-step hints that explain the reasoning before they place anything
• A glossary you can read any time

A DAILY HABIT
• A new daily challenge every day, stamped on a calendar
• Shields protect your streak when a day gets away from you
• Monthly medals for a complete month

BUILT FOR REAL HANDS
• One-hand mode with a side pad, left or right
• High-contrast board and three text sizes
• Light, dark and system themes

YOURS ALONE
• No account, nothing to sign up for
• Works offline — on a plane, underground, anywhere
• Every move is saved automatically, on this device

Stats track your best times by difficulty, your streaks, and which techniques you lean on, so you can watch yourself improve.
