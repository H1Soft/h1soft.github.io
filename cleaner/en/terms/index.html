03

Selection and confirmation

Duplicate and similar-photo classifications, keep recommendations, and estimated savings help you review files. They do not determine a file’s importance or replace your decision. Review files and settings before selecting an action.

Cleaning follows an explicit action from you. An Android system confirmation normally follows; if you have granted media-management access, some system confirmations may be simplified.

04

Bin and permanent deletion

Media cleaned through the app is first moved to the system bin and may be restored during its retention period. Phone Cleaner uses a 30-day period; the bin shows the remaining time.

Phone Cleaner cannot restore files after you permanently delete them from the bin or their retention period expires. The bin does not guarantee recovery after corruption, deletion by another app, or other operating-system actions. Back up important files before cleaning.

Bytes moved to the bin are not necessarily space immediately freed. Estimated amounts are identified as estimates; actual freed space is based on changes in device storage.

05

Shrinking photos and videos

Shrinking creates a new file. Resolution, quality, format, and some metadata may change according to the selected settings. Previews and estimated savings may differ from the final result depending on the device and file.

Shrinking alone does not automatically delete originals. Review the result and decide whether to move the originals to the bin. Files that cannot be encoded or do not provide savings may be skipped. Low storage, temperature, or operating-system limits may interrupt a job.

06

App archiving and uninstalling

Archiving removes an app’s main installation while retaining user data where supported. Restoring the app may require its installation store. Support and restoration depend on the device, app, and store.

Uninstalling may remove both the app and its associated data. The 30-day media-bin rule does not apply to app archiving or uninstalling. Review the information Android presents before proceeding.
