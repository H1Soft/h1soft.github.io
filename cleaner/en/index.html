A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
get started.

Are photos deleted automatically?+

No. Phone Cleaner finds items you can review, then you choose what to clear. Android asks for confirmation before moving items to the bin. The confirmation flow can differ if you have explicitly enabled quick-cleanup permission.

What happens to photo quality?+

Resolution and compression depend on your chosen settings. Compare the preview with the original and review estimated savings before applying. Take time to compare photos that matter to you.

Can I restore photos from the bin?+

You can restore photos during the 30-day retention period. They cannot be restored after you empty the bin or the retention period expires.

What does archiving an unused app mean?+

Archiving frees the space used by an app while keeping its data. Reinstall it later to pick up where you left off. Availability depends on your device, Android version and the app.

Which devices are supported?+
