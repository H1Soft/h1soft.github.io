What the recording demonstrates

  1. The user opens Shrink long videos, selects a file, and explicitly starts shrinking.
  2. The app displays conversion progress and pause/stop controls.
  3. The user leaves the app. An ongoing notification keeps the operation visible while the foreground service continues local conversion.

Foreground service types

Android 15 and later: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING performs user-requested photo/video encoding.

Android 11–14: the same user-visible local encoding flow uses FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC as the compatible service type. It is local media transcoding, not a network transfer.

The recording was captured on an Android 17 QA emulator with 16 KB pages. The service starts only for a user-requested conversion and stops when the job finishes or is canceled. Phone Cleaner does not request the Internet permission.
