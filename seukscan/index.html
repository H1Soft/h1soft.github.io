FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are scanned documents sent to a server?

Document scanning and text recognition run on your device. H1Soft does not collect your documents or recognized text on a server. When you choose to share, export or back up, files go to the app or location you select. Text recognition varies by device and document language.

Which export formats are supported?

Export PDF, JPG, PNG or text files. Choose PDF paper size and quality, and add an opening password or watermark.

Can I scan ID cards?

Use ID card and passport presets or split a book spread into pages. Organize scans with folders and favorites, and merge documents.

Can I scan multiple pages?

Add pages and refine your document with cropping, filters and rotation.

How can I contact support?

Email us with questions or bug reports. Include your device model and iOS version to help us investigate. Please do not send original documents containing sensitive information.
